# 05 · Scoring, Cairn & Daily

## Skip counting — decided

Adopted from the **Mackinac Island Stone Skipping Tournament** rule:

> Winner is determined strictly by number of bounces. Judges look for **clear daylight between the splashes.** If a stone begins to "patter" or glide seamlessly along the surface **without rising back into the air**, judges stop counting.

**A skip counts only when the rock leaves the water** — genuine air, visible gap. The moment the run degrades into a patter/glide, counting stops.

### Why this matters structurally

Without this rule, a fast flat throw farms a huge tail of tiny contacts while a powerful throw gets fewer big hops — two opposed strategies needing two leaderboards.

**With it, the flat-throw exploit is dead.** Counted skips require air, which requires energy retention, which is what produces distance. **Skips and distance become correlated.** One number, one leaderboard.

Vocabulary alignment: **plink** (heavy water between hops) counts; **pitty-pat** (light water between) stops the count.

## Score model — decided

- **Skips = the score.** Primary and ranked.
- **Distance = tiebreak**, displayed alongside.
- **Rejected:** skips × distance. Destroys legibility, double-counts a correlated signal, kills the live count-up.

## Cairn — decided

- **One cairn per day.**
- Sits **in the skipping lane, at the far end** of the open pond.
- **Both bearing and distance change daily**, rolled **independently**. No clamping on extreme combinations.
- **Bonus = ×2.**
- **Impact outcome depends on the hit**, driven by real physics and momentum — a glancing hit may let the rock continue, a solid hit stops it.

### Why a multiplier, not a flat bonus

A multiplier **preserves the ranking of skill**. A flat bonus would let a mediocre throw that clipped a cairn beat a clean long run. A multiplier amplifies a good throw but can't rescue a bad one. It also stays legible: `23 skips ×2 → 46`.

### What the daily reposition buys

If the cairn sat straight ahead at max range, players would throw max every time and aim would be trivial. Moving it makes each day a different line at near-max range — one seed value.

It also gives wind and chop a job: some days the cairn is downwind (a gift), some days across the chop (going for it costs you). **Wind and chop become the strategic layer.**

### Accepted consequences

- With independent rolls, extremes will occasionally align and **nobody reaches the cairn.** This is a feature — "nobody got it today" is a good shared moment. Do not clamp.
- On far-cairn days the ×2 is effectively veteran-only. Varying distance means it opens up on close days.

## Daily challenge — decided (revised)

- **Fixed global reset time** — everyone's day flips at the same instant worldwide.
- Universal cairn, shared conditions.
- **No sifting before the throw.** The daily hands every player the exact same rock — one universal spec, seeded fresh each day alongside the cairn and conditions. Nobody picks, nobody searches.
- **3 throws, the same rock, three times.** The purest possible isolation of throwing skill — the only variable across every player, and across a single player's own three throws, is execution.
- Sifting still exists (`02-gathering.md`) but is fully decoupled from the daily — a separate, unscored activity for sea glass, with no bearing on the daily rock.
- **Best of 3 counts.**

## Leaderboards — decided

| Board | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Today's scores, includes the cairn ×2 |
| All-time | Includes the ×2 |
| Personal best | The player's own record |

> Note: because cairn distance varies daily, an all-time board that includes the ×2 will tend to cluster records on close-cairn days. Accepted.

## Named outcomes

Real skipper vocabulary, useful as distinct result states.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Plink | Clean skip, heavy water between hops — counts |
| Pitty-pat | Short skips at end of run, light water — stops the count |
| Run | One full skip sequence |
| Plonk | Sinks on first contact |
| Skronker | Never touches water |
| Cresting out | Hits a wave top, takes a high angle, run dies |
